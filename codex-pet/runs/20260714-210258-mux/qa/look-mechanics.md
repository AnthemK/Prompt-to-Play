Mux look mechanics

- Character type: compact plush fox with a soft head-and-torso body, fixed planted feet, expressive eyes, and a large tail attached at the back.
- Natural motion: the eyes lead first, then the muzzle and head turn or tilt, then the upper torso follows slightly. The lower body and feet stay anchored. The tail lags subtly and never becomes the main directional cue.
- Up (000): eyes lift clearly, muzzle tips slightly upward, chin lifts a little, ears rise with a small alert tilt, torso stays centered.
- Right (090): pupils, nose tip, muzzle, and head all turn toward screen-right. More of the left cheek becomes visible; the right cheek compresses slightly. Tail follows with a mild lag.
- Down (180): eyes and muzzle angle downward, chin tucks, upper torso compresses slightly, ears soften lower.
- Left (270): pupils, nose tip, muzzle, and head all turn toward screen-left. More of the right cheek becomes visible; the left cheek compresses slightly. Tail follows with a mild lag.
- Diagonals: interpolate evenly between adjacent cardinals. Each 22.5-degree step should show similar-sized changes with no flips, snaps, or sudden scale shifts.
- Eye rules: preserve the original eye construction. Move the visible gaze with whole-eye and eyelid participation, not pasted-on googly pupils.
- Registration rules: keep the same body scale, baseline, and planted lower body across all 16 directions. Direction should read from face, head, ears, and slight upper-body turn rather than whole-sprite rotation.
