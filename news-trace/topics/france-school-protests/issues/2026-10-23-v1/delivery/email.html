Chronology, stakeholder positions and a progress review

This expanded thematic report separates school grievances, broader social mobilisation and incident-specific violence. It contains an 11-point timeline, a stakeholder map, an implementation review and competing explanations.

Where matters stand

The distinction between a promise, reported implementation and a verified outcome is central. The education response includes a staffing commitment, but whether the right teaching reached the right classrooms remains a separate question. Reuters’s account of the concession provides the baseline.

The negotiations also raise a representation question: LCP reports that student representatives were absent from the 21 October meeting with labour confederations. Read LCP’s reporting.

The latest section covers the changing protest restrictions and the limits of available legal evidence. It does not treat a local court development as a nationwide decision. Read the 23 October update.
